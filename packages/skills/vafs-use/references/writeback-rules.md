# Writeback Rules
Last updated: 2026-10-10

- Preserve raw, unprocessed material in the correct private Drive and register source identity, capture status and verified artifact links in SOURCES.md.
- Write unique generalized synthesis to knowledge/; write procedures to their cookbook/runbook owner.
- Keep references in REFERENCES.md and unresolved understanding in GAPS.md.
- Define research by living units; keep dated evidence in YYYY/MM-DD/ and log entries at most two lines.
- Keep goals in canonical Notion and templates beside generated content.
- Preserve source path, source URL, date, and author when available.
- Do not create duplicate pages when an existing canonical page should be updated.
- Do not commit directly to the default branch.
- Use branch and pull request for cleanup-agent changes.

These are AFS placement rules. The cancelled prototype's existing raw route and cleanup mocks do not implement Drive preservation or source ingestion.
